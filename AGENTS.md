# AgentResolver operating instruction

Internal cross-chat operating state is intentionally **not stored in this public repository**.

Authorized AgentResolver operators/agents with access to the private companion repository must read:

`waxsway/agentresolver-internal/PROJECT_STATE.md`

before making material architecture, pricing, payment-rail, discovery, distribution, or revenue changes.

## Hard product filter — Guard first

These rules are mandatory unless the canonical private project state explicitly records a later owner-approved strategic change.

1. **Guard is the product.** AgentResolver's primary product is the pre-sign x402 payment Guard. Free procurement/discovery, MCP surfaces, manifests, canaries, and legacy utilities are supporting infrastructure.
2. **No new utility endpoints.** Do not add another paid utility, deterministic microservice, or generic capability merely to create another place for AgentResolver to exist.
3. **No passive distribution work.** Do not submit to another directory, registry, marketplace, catalog, or discovery surface solely for visibility. A funded verifier that independently purchases Guard may be evaluated as a buyer-validation lane, but verifier purchases do not count as product demand.
4. **No speculative ecosystem builds.** Do not build AWS/Vercel/OpenSea/other marketplace integrations merely because the ecosystem supports them. Require concrete external demand or a validated payer before material runtime work.
5. **No unrequested evidence integrations.** Keep wallet-risk/reputation/AML evidence as an extension point. Do not merge CredScore or another evidence provider into Guard until real callers request that evidence.
6. **Demand must be buyer-side Guard use.** A qualified caller is an unrelated external agent/runtime that invokes Guard because it is about to pay a third-party target. Verifier bots, directories, marketplace audits, listing probes, self-payments, internal tests, and circular traffic are excluded.
7. **Repeat means repeat.** The current product-validation target is three unrelated qualified callers that each use Guard more than once. At least one should demonstrate willingness to pay materially more than the historical $0.001 probe price before higher-value pricing is treated as validated.
8. **Do not optimize for raw settlement count.** Settlement revenue remains real accounting revenue, but verifier/audit settlements are not evidence of organic Guard demand.
9. **Keep the README Guard-first.** Public positioning must lead with what Guard checks, the pre-sign hook, maintained integration guides, price, non-custodial boundary, and evidence limitations. Supporting infrastructure belongs below the Guard product story.
10. **Prefer conversations over surfaces.** The replacement for passive distribution is targeted validation with builders of wallet-capable x402 agents. Research and preparation are allowed; identity-bearing outreach still follows the owner's explicit-approval rules in private project state.

Public contributors should use the public source, README, OpenAPI, well-known discovery surfaces, tests, and production behavior as the product contract.

Do not add private operator state, revenue telemetry, internal strategy notes, credentials, wallet secrets, private keys, seed phrases, API credentials, or personal-account details to this public repository.

Commercial/runtime claims must be verified against current production evidence; HTTP 402 traffic alone is not revenue.
